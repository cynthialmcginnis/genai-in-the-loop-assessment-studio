Beyond AI Detection: Redesigning Authentic Assessments for the GenAI Era

Faculty start from an assessment designed before GenAI, name the learning it was built to show, and redesign it with an engineered GenAI pairing: a defined role for GenAI joined to a thinking move that students do themselves and that is graded.
