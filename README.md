## Use the Workshop-in-a-Box

1. Open a new chat in a GenAI tool your institution permits.
2. Attach the [Workshop-in-a-Box PDF](https://cynthialmcginnis.github.io/genai-in-the-loop-assessment-studio/workshop/materials/Beyond_AI_Detection_WIAB_Draft.pdf).
3. Send this prompt:

```
I attached a Workshop-in-a-Box PDF. Read the entire document before you reply. Treat its Section C as your operating instructions and follow them exactly. Start with the required welcome, then ask me the intake questions the document specifies. Work from this document only: do not search the web or bring in outside sources, and do not summarize the document. If you cannot read the whole PDF, tell me before you begin.
```

4. Answer the chat's questions about your participants, workshop length (30, 60, or 90 minutes), and format. The chat then builds the agenda and the workshop.

Short version, for tools that follow documents well:

```
I attached a Workshop-in-a-Box PDF. Follow its Section C operational instructions exactly, and start with the required welcome.
```

Faculty paste or upload (Word, PowerPoint with speaker notes, PDF, or text) any assessment (essay, problem set, lab, presentation, debate, role-play, case study, project, and more) and its learning outcomes. The studio:

- classifies each learning outcome by the thinking its verb asks for, and flags vague verbs;
- reads the assessment for features that make it easier or harder for GenAI to do the work without the learning;
- ranks twenty-four GenAI-in-the-loop designs (including games, role-plays, and challenges such as Stump the bot, Red-team your plan, The hot seat, Genre showdown, and Escape the vague) by fit, each with a hook written for the uploaded assignment, each with a copy-ready prompt filled with the faculty member's details, what students submit, how to grade, and a no-GenAI route;
- drafts a student-facing GenAI-use expectation;
- builds a deeper-analysis prompt to paste into the faculty member's own permitted GenAI tool;
- exports the plan as .html or .txt.

Everything runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored. It works offline once loaded.

Companion tool for the Workshop-in-a-Box "Beyond AI Detection: Redesigning Authentic Assessments for the GenAI Era," by Molly Barra and Cynthia McGinnis, USM Generative AI Pedagogy Fellowship Program, 2026-2027. Draft.

The analysis is rule-based. It suggests designs; it does not make any assessment secure or prove who did the work.
